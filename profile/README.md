# NeuroLIAA
The neuroLIAA is an interdisciplinary environment that combines different aspects of computational and cognitive neuroscience, computational psychiatry and data science, using different techniques such as EEG, MEG, eye tracking, modeling, among others.

We are part of the [Applied Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (LIAA)](https://www.liaa.dc.uba.ar/), based on the Departamento de Computación, Facultad de Ciencias Exactas y Naturales, Universidad de Buenos Aires and the Instituto de Investigación en Ciencias de la Computación (ICC), CONICET-UBA, Argentina.
